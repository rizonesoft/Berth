2. **P1. Shutdown needs a mandatory drain.** Exit currently drains the tray menu, disposes the icon, and shuts down the dispatcher. A timer can fire during that drain, and a restore already moving icons can outlive the dispatcher and lose its desktop worker during teardown. Stop accepting triggers when exit is requested, invalidate the pending timer, and await the admitted restore and any `Finished` delivery before releasing their dependencies. Cover alternate shutdown paths in `finally`. Test Exit with a gated restore and a retrigger pending. Release the gate and verify shutdown completes without a late event, a second restore, or a dispatcher deadlock. 3. **P2. Timer validation and restore admission must be atomic.** If a callback validates its generation, releases the lock to load settings, and then sets `_running`, a newer trigger or disposal can win in between. After loading settings, claim the still-current generation under the lock, capture its reason, and register the work that shutdown will drain before starting it. Invoke `Finished` outside the lock and include delivery in that tracked work. Force a cancelled callback to run while its replacement is pending. It must leave the replacement armed and produce no restore, event, or log line.

If the first restore finishes five seconds after the last trigger, the plan waits another three seconds. Record the last trigger’s deadline and run the follow-up once both conditions hold: the first restore has finished and the deadline has passed.
